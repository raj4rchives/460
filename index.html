Mission & Vision

Our Mission

We are committed to produce highly skilled professionals who are trained and guided by us in the field of research and technical education to serve the society. Our mission is to illuminate the lamp of knowledge in the nation for everyone who deserves to be well educated.

Our Vision

Our vision is to produce outstandingly capable, trained, conversant and skilful individuals having passion for technology with positive attitude and urge to become successful professional citizens. We provide excellent standard of quality technical education by keeping pace with rapidly changing technology.

Welcome to GP Kanpur

Government Polytechnic Kanpur is one of the most prestigious technical institutions in Uttar Pradesh. Established to impart high-quality diploma education, the institute has a sprawling campus with state-of-the-art infrastructure. We aim to produce skilled and competent engineers who can contribute effectively to the industrial growth of the nation.


Our experienced faculty, modern laboratories, and focus on practical learning ensure that our students are well-prepared for the challenges of the professional world.
